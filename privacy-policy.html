FractalLab - Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2026

1. Introduction

FractalLab ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with our services and content across social media platforms.

2. Information We Collect

FractalLab is primarily a content publishing service. We collect minimal information:

  • Platform Interaction Data: When you interact with our content on platforms like YouTube or TikTok, those platforms may share aggregated analytics (views, likes, comments) with us through their standard creator tools.
  • OAuth Authorization: When connecting to social media platforms for content publishing, we store OAuth tokens locally on our systems to maintain authorized access. These tokens are never shared with third parties.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information from viewers
  • Email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses
  • Payment or financial information
  • Browsing history or tracking data
  • Cookies or similar tracking technologies

4. How We Use Information

Any aggregated analytics data we receive from social media platforms is used solely to:

  • Improve our content quality and relevance
  • Understand audience engagement patterns
  • Optimize publishing schedules

5. Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens and API credentials are stored securely on local systems and are never transmitted to third parties. We use industry-standard security practices to protect all stored data.

6. Third-Party Platforms

Our content is distributed through third-party platforms including but not limited to YouTube, TikTok, Twitter/X, and Instagram. Each platform has its own privacy policy governing how they handle your data. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these platforms:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • YouTube/Google Privacy Policy

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Data Retention

We retain aggregated analytics data for content performance analysis. OAuth tokens are retained only as long as needed for platform access and are revoked when no longer required.

9. Your Rights

Since we collect minimal personal data, most data privacy rights are managed through the third-party platforms where you interact with our content. If you have concerns about any data we may hold, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our YouTube channel: FractalLab on YouTube.
